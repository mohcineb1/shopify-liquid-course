# Research notes — ch-62 SEO from the Template Layer

Verified 2026-08-14 from official sources.

- Shopify metadata: include title, meta description, and canonical in `<head>`; `page_title`, `page_description`, and global `canonical_url` support the theme pattern. Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/seo/metadata
- Shopify robots: every store has a default robots.txt; a `templates/robots.txt.liquid` customization can allow/disallow URLs, add crawl-delay, sitemap URLs, or block crawlers. It is unsupported by Shopify Support and errors can lose traffic. Preserve default `robots` object/Liquid behavior rather than plain-text replacement. Crawling and indexing differ: blocked pages can still be indexed. Default robots rules include cart, checkout, search, policies, and `/collections/*+*` filtered collections. Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/editing-robots-txt
- Google structured data: markup describes content on the page; do not create empty markup pages or mark information not visible to users. JSON-LD is generally recommended because it is easier to implement/maintain; supply fewer complete/accurate properties rather than broad inaccurate schema. Validate with Rich Results Test and monitor post-deploy reports. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data
- Google search gallery currently lists Article, Breadcrumb, Organization, and Product features; rich-result appearance is not guaranteed. FAQ is not listed as a current generic rich-result gallery feature, so teach it as structured data/semantic content without promising enhanced display [VERIFY] exact eligibility. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/search-gallery
